Saturn as antardasha lord directly activates your Lagna (the body itself). When the Lagna lord runs as sub-period, the body's inherent constitution becomes amplified. For Capricorn, that means Vata flares: dry skin, rough patches, flaking, possibly cracked heels or elbows. This is not random — it is your chart's timing.

Saturn is exalted in Libra (10th house). Libra is ruled by Venus — the planet of skin, beauty, and softness. Exalted Saturn sitting in Venus's sign creates friction: Saturn's dryness conflicts with Libra's natural desire for balance and glow. The result: skin that struggles to hold moisture even with good external care.
